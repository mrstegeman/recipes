From [Chelsey Amer](https://chelseyamernutrition.com/high-protein-pumpkin-mousse/).

## Ingredients

* 1 package Silken Tofu
* ½ cup pumpkin puree
* ¼ cup brown sugar
* ½ teaspoon cinnamon
* ⅛ teaspoon nutmeg
* ⅛ teaspoon ground ginger
* Pinch of salt

## Directions

* In a high-powered blender or food processor combine tofu, pumpkin puree, brown sugar, cinnamon,
  nutmeg, and ginger. Blend until smooth.
* Pour the mousse into a bowl. Cover and refrigerate for at least one hour.
* After the mousse thickens, divide into serving dishes and top with whipped cream and shaved
  chocolate, if desired. Enjoy!
